# Exercise 10: Post-Workshop Resource Cleanup

When you have finished exploring all exercises in this workshop, clean up your provisioned Azure AI Foundry resources to avoid incurring unnecessary Azure costs.

This exercise takes approximately **5-10** minutes.

---

<a id="cleanup"></a>
## Post-Workshop Resource Cleanup

To prevent ongoing charges on your Azure subscription after completing the workshop, clean up all provisioned resources.

### 1. Delete Deployed Models

1. In Visual Studio Code, refresh the **Azure Resources** view.
2. Expand the **Models** section.
3. Right-click your deployed model and select **Delete**.

### 2. Delete the Foundry Project & Resource Group

1. Open the [Foundry Portal](https://ai.azure.com), navigate to **Settings**, and select **Delete project**.
2. Alternatively, log into the [Azure Portal](https://portal.azure.com).
3. Navigate to **Resource Groups**, select your project's resource group, click **Delete resource group**, and confirm deletion.
